## 6. LIMITATIONS SCREEN — Cannot Test
*Shown on the dedicated Limitations & Disclaimers page accessible from the main menu.*

This tool cannot test:
- **Identity** — Whether the compound is what the label states
- **Purity** — Chemical purity or absence of impurities
- **Potency** — Concentration, activity, or dosage accuracy
- **Sterility** — Freedom from microorganisms or bioburden
- **Endotoxins** — Bacterial endotoxin or pyrogen levels
- **Submicron particles** — Particles too small to see with a smartphone camera
- **Chemical degradation** — Oxidation, hydrolysis, or other invisible changes

Cases where accuracy is lower:
- Dark-tinted or amber glass vials — particles may not be visible through the glass
- Colored or naturally turbid liquids — cloudiness may be expected, not a defect
- Suspensions and emulsions — intended to appear cloudy or particulate
- Lyophilized powders or cake products — appearance varies and is not a liquid clarity screen
- Very small vials (< 1mL) — difficult to frame and assess accurately
- Poor lighting, blur, glare, or motion in captures — reduces all analysis accuracy
- Labels printed over the glass body — interferes with clarity assessment

What a "Pass" Result Means:
> No obvious visual issue was detected in the captured images under these specific conditions. This does not mean the product is safe, authentic, uncontaminated, or fit for any intended use. A pass is a negative screening result only.
